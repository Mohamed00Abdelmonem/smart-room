## 1. The Industry Challenge in Furniture Retail

Selling furniture online is fundamentally different from selling fashion or electronics:
1. **Scale & Fit Uncertainty**: Over 38% of online furniture returns happen because customers cannot gauge true 1:1 physical dimensions in their living rooms.
2. **Generic Templates Fail Luxury Brands**: Standard platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce) offer rigid templates that look generic, lack architectural elegance, and fail to convey the tactile craftsmanship of fine woods, fabrics, and marbles.
3. **High Development Costs & Delays**: Hiring a software agency to build a custom multi-page e-commerce website costs between **\$15,000 to \$50,000** and takes 3 to 6 months.
4. **Disjointed Regional Payment & Logistics**: Most website builders lack native support for regional checkout infrastructure like **InstaPay (IPN)**, **ValU BNPL installments**, and white-glove in-home room assembly.

## 7. How to Get Started in 3 Steps

1. **Navigate to "My Furniture Store"**:
   Click **"MY STORE"** in the top navigation bar of the VESTA-AI platform ([http://localhost:5173/my-store](http://localhost:5173/my-store)).
2. **Customize Your Design in the Builder**:
   Open the **Website Builder** ([http://localhost:5173/my-store/builder](http://localhost:5173/my-store/builder)). Choose a starting template (*Palazzo Luxury Atelier* or *Nordic Calm Living*), upload your brand logo, and adjust your catalog pieces.
3. **Publish to the World**:
   Click **Publish Store**. Your store is immediately live to shoppers at:
   `https://vestaai.com/store/your-brand-slug`
