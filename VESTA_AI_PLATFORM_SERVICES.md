## 🏛️ Service 1: AI Spatial Visualizer & Room Studio
*Turn a customer's smartphone room photo into an architecturally calibrated luxury interior in under 15 seconds.*

- **Upload & Automated Spatial Diagnostics**:
  - Customers snap a photo of their living room, bedroom, salon, or terrace.
  - VESTA AI automatically analyzes wall boundaries, floor perspective, natural window light angles, and ceiling heights.
  - Instant validation alerts for optimal angle, low light, or obstructed walkways.
- **True 1:1 Scale Spatial Calibration**:
  - Eliminates scale ambiguity by rendering furniture pieces in exact metric dimensions (`cm` / `m`).
  - Active dimensional callouts (e.g., `1.45 m Walkway Clearance`, `140 cm Coffee Table Width`, `2.9 m Ceiling`).
- **Multi-Proposal Generation**:
  - Generates multiple distinct design concepts per room (e.g., *Modern Minimalist*, *Warm Contemporary*, *Nordic Serenity*, *Italian Classic Grandeur*).
  - Side-by-side **Interactive Before/After Split Slider** comparing the customer's raw empty room to the furnished masterpiece.
- **One-Click Furniture Swap & Try In Room**:
  - Swap sofas, marble coffee tables, accent armchairs, lighting fixtures, and wool rugs in real time.

## 🛋️ Service 2: Interactive Spatial Showroom & Physics Drop Simulator
*A sensory, cinema-grade 3D shopping experience that makes furniture tangible online.*
